Rail Support

Rail is a wardrobe app: photograph your clothes, get a look for the day, and check a purchase against what you already own.

Contact

To reach us, leave a request here. Add the Support ID from Settings in the app (tap it to copy) so we can find your records; do not post photos or personal details in a public request.

Subscription

  • Cancel or change. In the app: Settings › Manage or cancel subscription. Or on your iPhone: Settings › your name › Subscriptions › Rail. Cancel at least 24 hours before the end of the trial or period to avoid the next charge.
  • Restore. New phone or reinstall: Settings › Restore purchases, signed in with the same Apple Account.
  • Refunds. Purchases are billed by Apple. Request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Tokens

Tokens pay for AI work: 1 per photo and 1 per piece found in it, 1 for a studio photo, 2 to see a look on a model. If a task fails, its tokens are returned automatically. Your balance is in Settings.

Adding clothes

  • One photo of you in an outfit, or of clothes laid out, works best in good light with the whole piece visible.
  • A photo usually takes 30–60 seconds. If a piece fails, tap Try again on it.
  • If a category or colour is wrong, tap the piece in your closet and change it.

Your data

Your closet is stored on your phone. Photos are sent for AI processing only after you agree; you can switch this off or delete the server's records in Settings. Details are in the privacy policy.
