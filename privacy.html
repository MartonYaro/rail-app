Rail Privacy Policy

Effective 11 November 2026. Rail is published by its developer ("we").

The short version

Rail has no account and does not ask for your name, email or phone number. Your closet lives on your phone. Photos leave your phone only when you ask the app to process them with AI, and only after you agree in the app. We do not sell your data and we do not track you across other apps or websites.

What we process and why

  • Photos you choose. When you add clothes, ask for a studio photo or ask to see a look on a model, the photos you picked are sent to our server and passed to our AI processing provider, WaveSpeed AI, which finds the pieces and creates the pictures using AI models, including models from Google. We use them only to return the result to you. Our server does not keep the image files themselves; it keeps the task record with links to the uploaded photo and to each result, so the app can download the result. The provider stores the files for a limited time under its own terms. Photos are not used to train AI models by us.
  • An anonymous ID. The app creates a random identifier on your phone. It is how our server knows your token balance and which results are yours. It is not linked to your name or Apple Account.
  • Token balance and usage. We keep a record of tokens granted and spent for that ID, so balances are correct and refunds for failed tasks work.
  • Approximate location, if you allow it. Used to get the local weather for outfit suggestions. Rounded coordinates are sent from your phone to the weather service and are not sent to or stored on our server.
  • Purchases. Subscriptions are sold and billed by Apple. We receive confirmation that a subscription is active, not your payment details. We may use a subscription service provider (Adapty) to validate purchases and grant tokens.
  • Notifications, if you allow them. A reminder before a free trial ends is scheduled on your phone. No push token is sent to us.

What we do not do

No advertising, no advertising identifiers, no third-party analytics, no tracking across apps or websites, and no sale of personal data.

What stays on your phone

Your closet, the saved pictures of your pieces, your looks, what you wore and when, prices you entered and your answers to the first-run questions are stored on your device and are not uploaded.

Your choices

  • You can turn AI processing off at any time in Settings inside the app. Nothing is sent after that.
  • You can delete the data our server holds for your ID in Settings › Delete server data.
  • Deleting the app removes everything stored on your phone.
  • You can switch off location and notifications in the iOS Settings app.

How long we keep it

Task records and result links stay on our server until you delete them in the app. The token record for an anonymous ID is kept while the app is in use, so that your balance stays correct, and is removed on request.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct or delete your data, or to object to its processing. Most of this you can do in the app; for anything else, contact us and include the Support ID from the app's Settings, since we have no other way to find your records.

Children

Rail is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their data.
